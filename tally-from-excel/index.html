From an Excel register to Tally

Bills made in another program, or a register kept in Excel? Fill our template, one row per voucher, and convert it to the same Tally files.

The columns

ColumnWhat to typeExample
DateDD/MM/YYYY05/11/2026
Voucher TypeSales, Purchase, Credit Note, Debit Note, Receipt or PaymentSales
Voucher NoBill, note or receipt numberINV/26-27/101
Party, Party StateCustomer or supplierShree Traders
Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST, Round OffFor bills and notes37400 · 0 · 0 · 1870 · 0
TotalBill total, or the amount of a receipt or payment39270
Cash or BankFor receipts and paymentsBank
Against Bill NoFor receipts and payments: the bill they settleINV/26-27/101
NarrationAny note (write "purchase" in it for a debit note on a purchase)

How each row is booked

  • Sales: party Dr total; Sales, Output CGST/SGST/IGST Cr; round off either side.
  • Purchase: Purchase, Input CGST/SGST/IGST Dr; party Cr total.
  • Credit Note: the reverse of a sale (goods returned by a customer).
  • Debit Note: an extra charge to a customer, or, with "purchase" in the narration, goods returned to a supplier.
  • Receipt: Cash or Bank Dr, party Cr. Payment: party Dr, Cash or Bank Cr.

If a bill's taxable value plus taxes plus round off doesn't equal its total, the row is refused with its row number, because Tally would refuse the voucher.

Worked example

DateTypeNoPartyTaxableIGSTTotalBank
05/11/2026SalesINV/26-27/101Shree Traders37,4001,87039,270
06/11/2026ReceiptRC/26-27/014Shree Traders20,000Bank

Two vouchers: the sale leaves Shree Traders owing ₹39,270; the receipt, against INV/26-27/101, brings it down to ₹19,270 in Tally's bill-wise outstanding.

TallyPrime (Release 4.0 and later) can also import Excel files itself, with its own templates or mapping. Use whichever suits your accountant.
